# Provenance

Private distribution. The bounded torrent byte parser and selected exact-resolver flow derive from JackTorr by Sam-Max and contributors (upstream https://github.com/Sam-Max/plugin.video.jacktorr), including the AM6B release317 adaptations. The upstream MIT notice is retained in LICENSE. bobbi adaptations use the project's GPL-3.0 distribution terms.

Only explicitly whitelisted source files from the installed plugin.video.jacktorr 1.2.2+am6b.317 were inspected. No profile, settings, credentials, binaries, backups or media were imported. The old service, player observer, source browser and engine-management functions are intentionally not included. No service extension or native library is provided.
